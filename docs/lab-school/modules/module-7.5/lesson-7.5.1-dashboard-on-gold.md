# Lesson 7.5.1: Dashboard on gold

Module 7.5: Analytics dashboard

## Concept
Stand up a dashboard tool, such as Metabase or Streamlit, on top of the gold layer so analysts can
explore the data without writing SQL. The gold layer's clean, modeled tables are exactly what make
self serve analytics possible; you cannot build a trustworthy dashboard on messy raw data. This is
the consumption end of the pipeline, where all the refinement finally turns into something people use.

## Why it matters
"I put a dashboard on the gold layer so analysts can self serve, the consumption end of the pipeline."
