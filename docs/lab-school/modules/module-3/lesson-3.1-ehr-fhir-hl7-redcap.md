# Lesson 3.1: EHR, FHIR, HL7, REDCap

Module 3: Synthetic healthcare data (Synthea, FHIR, HL7)

## Concept
EHR is the electronic health record system, and Epic is the dominant one. FHIR is the modern
standard for exchanging health data, REST and JSON based and organized into resources like Patient,
Encounter, and Observation. HL7 v2 is the older pipe delimited messaging standard still common
inside hospitals. Epic exposes its data through reporting databases: Clarity, a relational store for
batch reporting, and Caboodle, a data warehouse that powers Epic's Cogito analytics. REDCap is a
research electronic data capture tool, common in academic medical centers. Knowing these names
signals an understanding of the data platform side of a health system, not just the clinical side.

## Why it matters
"I can place the healthcare data standards: FHIR for modern exchange, HL7 v2 for legacy messaging,
Epic Clarity and Caboodle for reporting, and REDCap for research capture."
