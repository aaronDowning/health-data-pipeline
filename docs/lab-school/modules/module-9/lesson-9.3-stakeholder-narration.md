# Lesson 9.3: Stakeholder narration

Module 9: Capstone and demo

## Concept
The same system, told in plain English for a non technical audience: what problem it solves, what the
data is, and why the result is trustworthy, with no jargon. This is the second half of the dual
explain back habit the whole lab trains, and it is what makes the work legible to the people who fund
and use it. A system you can only explain to engineers is half a system.

## Why it matters
"I can narrate the system in plain English for a non technical audience, the so what with no jargon."
