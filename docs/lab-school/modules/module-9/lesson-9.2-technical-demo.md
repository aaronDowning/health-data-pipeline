# Lesson 9.2: Technical demo

Module 9: Capstone and demo

## Concept
A walkthrough for engineers, kept under ten minutes, that moves through every layer in depth: ingest,
the document and relational stores, orchestration, distributed processing, the quality gates, and
CI/CD. The goal is to explain why each piece exists and how it works, not just that it runs. The
ability to defend any layer under a technical question is what separates a built project from a
borrowed one.

## Why it matters
"I can give a technical demo that explains every layer in depth, not just that it runs."
